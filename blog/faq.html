Frequently Asked Questions About This Test

Have questions about how your score is calculated, privacy safety, or which test edition is right for you? Here are clear, straightforward answers to the most common questions our users ask.

Q1 Is my test data saved or shared with anyone?

No, absolutely not. Your privacy is guaranteed. All checkbox selections and calculated scores are processed 100% locally inside your web browser. We do not store, track, or send your individual test answers to any server.

Q2 How is my final score calculated?

You start with a total score of 100 points. Every item you check off subtracts 1 point from 100. If you check 15 boxes, your final score will be 85. If you check 0 boxes, your score remains 100.

Q3 What are the differences between the 5 test editions?

We offer five distinct sets of rice purity questions, one edition for each audience and setting:

  • Standard Edition: The classic 100-item life experience checklist.
  • College Edition (100 Qs): 100 questions specifically tailored for university life, dorm experiences, all-nighters, exam stress, and campus events.
  • Clean & Safe Edition: A 100% PG family-friendly checklist focusing on school, hobbies, and harmless life events.
  • Couples Edition: Questions specifically designed for romantic partners to take together and share past memories.
  • Wild Adventure Edition: Focused on travel, thrill-seeking, extreme sports, and spontaneous outdoor adventures.

Q4 Can I save or download my score result?

Yes! Once you click "Calculate My Score", a modal window will pop up showing your numerical score, tier badge, and achievement icon. You can click Download Card to save an image or share directly on WhatsApp and Twitter (X).

Q5 Can I retake the test or clear my answers?

Yes, at any time! Click the Clear Checkboxes button at the top or bottom of the page to uncheck all items and reset your score back to 100 instantly, so you can run a fresh rice purity check any time.

Q6 Is there a "good" or "bad" score?

Not at all! The test is purely for fun and lighthearted self-reflection. A high score means you've had a safe and wholesome journey, while a lower score means you've gathered many wild stories. Both are worthy of celebrating!

Q7 What is a rice purity score?

A rice purity score is the number you are left with after subtracting the boxes you checked from 100. A high score means fewer listed experiences apply to you; a low score means more of them do. Either way it is just a count, not a judgement.

Q8 How does the rice purity test work?

You work through 100 statements and tick every one that applies to you. Each tick subtracts one point from 100, and the number left at the end is your score. There is no timer, no sign-up, and your answers stay in your own browser.

Q9 Is the rice purity test safe to take online?

Yes. The test itself is safe and anonymous: there is no account, no name or email required, and your answers are calculated in your own browser and never stored or shared. One honest note: the Standard Edition questions cover mature topics, so it is written for adults. Younger players usually pick our Clean & Safe Edition instead, which keeps every question mild and family-friendly.

Q10 Is the rice purity test accurate?

No, and it never claims to be. It is not a scientific or psychological test, and there is no official dataset behind it. It is a fun self-reflection checklist: the number counts how many of the 100 listed experiences apply to you, nothing more. Treat it as entertainment, not a measurement of your character or your life.

Q11 Is a higher or lower rice purity test score better?

Neither. A higher score simply means fewer of the listed experiences apply to you, and a lower score means more of them do. The test does not rank people, and no score is more virtuous or more interesting than another. It is a snapshot of one moment in your life, not a verdict on it.

Q12 What does MPS mean in the rice purity test?

MPS stands for Member of the Preferred Sex. It is the test's old-fashioned, gender-neutral way of saying "the person you are attracted to," used in questions like "showered with a MPS" so the same question works for everyone.

Q13 What does "kissed horizontally" mean?

It means kissing while lying down, rather than standing or sitting up. It describes body position only. It does not imply that anything further happened — the test asks about further steps in separate questions.

Q14 What does streaking mean in the rice purity test?

Streaking means running naked in a public place, usually as a prank or a dare. On the test it sits in the wilder, rule-breaking section of the list, alongside things like skinny-dipping and run-ins with the police.

Q15 What does question 31 on the rice purity test mean?

On the classic 100-question list, question 31 asks whether you have had an orgasm due to someone else's manipulation. A warning, though: question numbers shift on rewritten and modern versions of the test, so if your version numbers its questions differently, the wording of the question itself is what counts, not the number.

Q16 What does question 69 on the rice purity test mean?

On the classic list, question 69 is listed simply as "69" — the well-known position. As with question 31, numbering varies between versions, so check the wording on the version you took rather than trusting the number alone.

Q17 How many questions are in the rice purity test?

100. Every question is worth exactly one point, and the test is scored out of 100: your score is 100 minus the number of boxes you checked. Check 20 boxes and you score 80; check none and you score 100.

Q18 What is a normal rice purity test score?

There is no official "normal" score, because no official dataset exists. As a rough, self-reported guide only, most college-aged people tend to land somewhere around 50 to 85, with younger people scoring higher and older people scoring lower simply because they have had more time to collect experiences. Any score in or outside that range is still just one person's story.

What Is the Rice Purity Test?

I first took the Rice Purity Test during my college orientation week, sitting in a dorm room with my roommate, both of us laughing at the questions and comparing scores by the end of the night. That's really what this test is: a simple checklist quiz, not a real exam or anything official. If you've never taken it before and are wondering what is a Rice Purity Test, think of it as a casual purity exam that measures life experience, not intelligence or morals.

It goes back to 1924 at Rice University in Houston, Texas, where students made a short survey to see how "pure" someone was based on their life experiences. By the 1980s, it had grown into the full 100-question version people take today, sometimes just called the rice purity score quiz by students who share results in group chats.

It was originally made for freshmen during Orientation Week, meant as a fun icebreaker to help new students bond instead of the usual awkward small talk.

Taking it is simple. You check the boxes next to anything you've experienced from the list, and once you finish all 100 rice purity test questions, you get a score between 0 and 100. A higher score usually means fewer boxes checked, so more "innocent." A lower score means more life experiences checked off.

There's no good or bad number here. When I took it, I scored in the low 70s, and by my second year it had dropped, which just reflected normal college life, new experiences and all. Most online purity test versions don't ask for your name or email, so it's safe and anonymous to take — which is exactly why so many people search "the rice purity test" instead of a random personality quiz that wants your details. When you finish, your rice purity test results are just one number — and if you are wondering about the rice purity test meaning behind that number, the chart below explains each range in plain language.

How Does the Rice Purity Test Work?

Taking this rice purity quiz online takes about five minutes, and the rules could not be simpler. Work your way through the 100 statements in the checklist above and tick every box that describes something you have actually done. Be honest — nobody sees your answers, and the result is only interesting if it is true. When you reach the end, press Calculate My Score and your rice purity score appears instantly.

The scoring is plain subtraction. You begin with 100 points and lose exactly one point for every box you checked. Tick 8 boxes and you score 92. Tick 41 boxes and you score 59. There is no timer, no pass mark and no ranking against other people. Your progress bar at the top of the page keeps count as you go, and your answers are saved on your own device, so you can close the page and finish the test later without starting again. In short, it is a simple rice purity check: tick what applies, subtract from 100, and your score is ready. This rice purity test online version runs entirely in your browser — no sign-up, no download.

Five editions, one simple idea

Most people start with the Standard Rice Purity Test above — the classic list of rice purity questions, organised into school and campus life, everyday mishaps, social events and nightlife, and wild adventures and romance. If you want a different flavour, the edition tabs switch the whole checklist: the College Edition is built around dorm life, exams and campus socials; the Clean & Safe Edition keeps every question mild and shareable, which makes it popular with younger players and families; the Couples Edition focuses on crushes, first dates and relationship milestones; and the Wild & Adventure Edition leans into travel, thrills and unforgettable nights out. Every edition uses the same 0-to-100 scoring, so scores from different editions are easy to compare with friends.

Why do people take the Rice Purity Test?

A century after it began as an orientation-week icebreaker, the rice purity test survives because it is a perfect conversation starter. Friends often just call it the pure test for short, and they compare scores in group chats, couples use it to swap stories they have never told each other, and students retake it each year to watch the number drift as university life happens to them. It works because the questions do the talking: every checked box is a memory, and every score is really just a short story about a life so far. It is also completely private here — no sign-up, no email, no posting your answers anywhere unless you choose to share your result card yourself.

Rice Purity Test FAQs

What is the Rice Purity Test?

The Rice Purity Test is a 100-question self-reflection checklist that started at Rice University in Houston, Texas. You tick every life experience on the list that applies to you, and your score is 100 minus the number of boxes you checked. It is a fun icebreaker about life experience — not an exam, a medical test, or a judgement of your character.

What is a rice purity score?

A rice purity score is simply the number you are left with after the subtraction: 100 minus the boxes you checked. It is not a grade — it only says how many of the listed experiences apply to you, and nothing about your character.

How is my Rice Purity Score calculated?

Your Rice Purity Score is simple subtraction. You start at 100 and lose one point for every statement you check. Check 12 boxes and your score is 88. Check 55 boxes and your score is 45. There is no weighting and no hidden algorithm — every question counts exactly the same.

What is a good Rice Purity Score?

There is no good or bad Rice Purity Score, because the test measures experience, not quality of character. As a rough guide, scores between 90 and 100 usually mean very few of the listed experiences, 60 to 89 is the range many students land in, and scores below 60 simply reflect a wider range of experiences. You can read our full guide on what counts as a good score for a detailed, honest breakdown.

What is the average Rice Purity Score by age?

Scores usually drift down as people get older, simply because life adds experiences over time. In casual, self-reported comparisons, high-school students often score in the 80s and 90s, college students commonly land somewhere in the 60s to 80s, and adults in their late twenties and beyond often sit lower. These are informal patterns people report, not scientific statistics — your own number depends on your life, not your birth year.

Is the Rice Purity Test anonymous?

Yes. The test on this page runs entirely in your browser. Your answers are not sent to a server, you do not need an account, and we never ask for your name or email. Your progress is only saved on your own device so you can come back to it, and you can clear it at any time with the Clear Checkboxes button.

What do the different test editions mean?

Alongside the Standard Rice Purity Test you will find four themed editions of 100 questions each: a College Edition focused on campus life, a Clean and Safe edition with milder everyday questions that anyone can share anywhere, a Couples Edition about relationships and romantic milestones, and a Wild and Adventure edition about travel, thrills and nightlife. Each edition is scored the same way, from 0 to 100.

Can I retake the Rice Purity Test?

Yes, as often as you like. Many people retake the test every year or two to see how their score changes as they collect new experiences. Use the Clear Checkboxes button to start again from a blank list, or try a different edition for a fresh set of 100 questions.

Why is my score different from my friend's score?

Because the test only reflects the specific experiences on its list. Two people the same age can have completely different scores depending on where they grew up, what their social life looks like, and which chances came their way. A difference in score says nothing about who is better, smarter or more mature — it just means you have lived different lives so far.

Contact Us

We Actually Read Every Message

This isn't some corporate inbox that nobody checks. It's just us, the small team behind Rice Purity Scores, and when a message comes in, one of us actually reads it and responds. Whether it's a quick question about how the test works, a bug you spotted, or just a random hello, we're genuinely happy to hear from you.

Your feedback has shaped a lot of what this site looks like today. Small things people mentioned, like the test loading slowly on older phones or a question being worded confusingly, have all led to real changes on our end.

You can reach us through the form below, by email directly, or through our social pages linked at the bottom of the site.

What People Usually Reach Out About

Most messages we get fall into a handful of categories, so here's a quick idea of what we're happy to help with.

  • General Questions. If you're confused about how the scoring works, why certain questions are worded a certain way, or anything else about the test itself, just ask. We'll explain it as clearly as we can.
  • Technical Issues. If a page isn't loading properly, a button isn't working, or something looks broken on your device, let us know. Mentioning your phone or browser (like Chrome on an Android, or Safari on an iPhone) helps us track down the issue faster.
  • Content Suggestions. Got an idea to make the site clearer or more useful? We actually read these and have made changes based on visitor suggestions before.
  • Privacy Questions. If you're unsure about what data we collect or how cookies work on this site, feel free to ask. We take this seriously, especially since a lot of our visitors are teens and younger college students.
  • Business or Partnership Inquiries. If you're reaching out about a collaboration, feature request, or media inquiry, send us the details and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

Sending Us a Message

If you'd rather use a form instead of email, here's how it usually works on our site:

Head to the Contact page and scroll down to the message form. Fill in your name (optional) and your email address (required, so we can actually reply). Write your message in the text box, the more specific you are, the faster we can help. Hit submit, and that's it, no confirmation emails or extra steps.

We typically get back to people within a day or two, though it can take a little longer if we're catching up on a backlog.

Prefer Emailing Us Directly?

If forms aren't your thing, that's completely fine. You can reach us directly through email instead, and honestly, a good chunk of our messages come in this way rather than through the contact form.

You can email us at admin@ricepurityscores.com.

We check this inbox regularly and try to reply within one to two business days. During busier weeks it might take a bit longer, but we always get back to everyone eventually.

A Few Things Worth Knowing Before You Write

We like to be upfront about a couple of things so there's no confusion:

  • This site runs independently and isn't officially affiliated with any university. We can't answer questions specific to campus policies or academic matters.
  • We don't keep records of anyone's test results. If you're asking us to look up a past score, we genuinely won't have it, everything here is anonymous by design and nothing gets saved on our end.
  • If your message is about something legal or related to content usage, mention that clearly in your subject line so we can prioritize it properly.

We're Genuinely Listening

Whether you're visiting for the first time or you've been back a few times, your message matters to us. This isn't some automated system pretending to care, it's a small group of people who built this site and actually want it to be useful for the people using it.

If something's on your mind, don't overthink it, just reach out.

Message sent! We'll get back to you soon.

Contact Us

Getting in Touch

The fastest way to reach us is email. Write to us at admin@ricepurityscores.com, or use the form below — the form doesn't send anything by itself; when you press the button it opens your own email app with your message already addressed and written out, ready for you to send. Either way, your message lands in the same inbox.

We do read what comes in. Corrections and bug reports in particular are genuinely useful — a site like this stays accurate because readers point at the things we've missed. What we won't do is promise you a reply time. This is a small site run alongside everything else in life, and while most messages do get an answer, it can take a while, and a question that's already covered in our guides may get a shorter reply pointing you to the page that answers it.

Before You Write — Three Quick Answers

A fair share of the messages we receive ask one of these three things, so here they are up front, in case it saves you an email:

  • "Can you look up my score?" We can't — and that's by design, not by policy. Your answers are counted inside your own browser and never reach us, so there is no record of your result anywhere for us to find. If you closed the tab, the score went with it.
  • "Can a question be changed or removed?" Possibly, and it's worth writing in. The Standard edition follows the traditional 100-question list, so we don't rewrite that lightly, but wording problems, broken translations and genuine mistakes do get fixed — tell us which question, in which edition, and what looks wrong.
  • "Can I use your questions or guides on my own site or video?" Ask first, please. Short quotes with a link back are usually fine; copying a full question list or guide wholesale is not. The details are in our Terms and Conditions.

What People Usually Write About

  • Corrections. A wrong fact in a guide, a score range that doesn't match the chart, a link that goes nowhere — send it, with the page address if you can.
  • Question suggestions. The themed editions grow and change over time, and ideas for new questions — or better wording for old ones — are welcome.
  • Something broken. If a page looks wrong on your phone or the score button does nothing, tell us which device and browser you were using. That detail turns a mystery into a fixable bug.
  • Business and media. Partnerships, advertising, licensing or press questions can all go to the same inbox — put "Business" in the subject line so it stands out.
  • Privacy questions. If anything in our Privacy Policy is unclear, ask us directly and we'll explain it in plain words.

Send a Message

Fill this in and press the button — your email app will open with everything filled in, addressed to admin@ricepurityscores.com. If nothing opens (some browsers block it), just copy the address and write to us the normal way.

A Note on What Not to Send

Please don't email us your test answers or a screenshot of your result list expecting us to interpret it personally — your score is yours, and our score meaning guide will walk you through it better than we could in an email. And if you're a student with a question about your actual university — housing, orientation week, campus rules — we're the wrong inbox entirely; we run a quiz website, and we're not affiliated with Rice University or any other school.
